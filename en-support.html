Common questions

Where is my drinking data stored?

Only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is included in your iPhone’s own backup like other app data. You can export it any time from Settings → Export my data (CSV).

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your logs move with your iPhone backup, not with your purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Mostly Dry and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal or trial end date.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is a standard drink?

A fixed amount of alcohol: 14 g in the US (a 12 oz beer at 5%, a 5 oz glass of wine or a 1.5 oz shot), 8 g for a UK unit, 13 g in Mexico and 10 g elsewhere. You can change it in Settings.

How do I add the widget or log from the Lock Screen?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Mostly Dry. With Pro, start a night out from the app to get a Lock Screen counter with a +1 button.

Is this a medical app?

No. Mostly Dry is a personal tracker, not medical advice or treatment. If you want to talk to someone about drinking, the app’s Get support page lists free, confidential helplines.
