Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what the Mostly Dry iPhone app (called Casi Seco in Spanish) does with your data. Mostly Dry is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: your drinking data never leaves your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. We collect a few anonymous usage statistics that contain no drinking information.

Your drink logs stay on your iPhone

Everything you log (drinks, dry days, your budget and goals, your typical week, resets and custom drinks) is stored only on your iPhone. The home-screen widget and the Lock Screen counter read it on the device. We never receive it, and it is not synced to iCloud by the app. It is included in your iPhone’s own backup, like other app data. Recap images and data exports are created on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them.

We do not collect consumer health data. Because your logs never leave your device, we have no copy to sell, share, or disclose.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used. The app sends only these events: when it is installed, opened or closed; which onboarding step was reached (a number), when the purchase screen is shown and where from, when a purchase is started or completed and its plan type, restores, whether you turned on reminders, and when you start a night out or a reset, share a recap image or export your data. The events never contain drink counts, dry days, budgets, goals, your answers about drinking, or any text you type. They include the app version, device model, iOS version and language, and are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email or Apple ID. PostHog stores these events in the United States. It uses your IP address only to estimate an approximate location (country and city), then discards it; we configure it not to keep IP addresses. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

Reminders

The evening check-in, the January Reset reminder and the trial reminder are scheduled on your device. Their text never mentions alcohol on your Lock Screen, and no notification data is sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Because they are anonymous we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can.

Adults only

Mostly Dry is for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It is not directed at children or teenagers, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
